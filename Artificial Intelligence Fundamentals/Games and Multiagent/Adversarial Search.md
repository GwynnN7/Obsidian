# Adversarial Search

**Two-player zero-sum games:** two players whose goals are diametrically opposed.

Characteristics:

- **Adversarial search:** the search accounts for the actions of an opponent.
- **Turn-based** play.
- **Perfect information:** the environment is fully observable to both players.
- Utility is evaluated from the perspective of the maximizing player (**MAX**).

## Game tree

States are nodes, actions are edges, with the game graph superimposed onto the tree. The tree may be **infinite**, because of an infinite state space or repeated states.
