# Social Welfare

A **societal** view of the agents, rather than individual utility.

## Pareto optimality

A state is **Pareto optimal** if any change to the outcome would make at least one player lose utility.

A non-Pareto-optimal outcome is a **waste of utility**: someone could be made better off without harming anyone.

## Utilitarian social welfare

Welfare = the **sum** of all players' utilities for a given outcome. Simple, but blind to **inequality** in the distribution.

## Egalitarian social welfare

Focuses on the **distribution** of utility: maximize the utility of the **worst-off** player (max-min criterion).

The **Gini coefficient** is a common metric for the spread of utility.
