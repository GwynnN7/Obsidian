# Reinforcement Learning

Learning with a right/wrong critic instead of step-by-step targets.

- The algorithm learns a **policy**: how to act given an observation of the world.
- Every action has an impact on the environment, and the environment gives feedback that guides the learning.
- Aimed at adaptation in autonomous systems and at decision making.

Related: [[Supervised Learning]] (which instead needs a target for every example), [[Agent Architectures]] and [[Monte-Carlo Tree Search]] (where a learned playout policy drives the search).
